Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-14

This site lets you upload a Minecraft resource pack or .jar file and generates a 3D "megablock" model from its textures. Everything happens entirely in your browser: the file you upload is read and processed locally using JavaScript, and is never sent to, or stored on, any server. Closing the tab discards it completely.

What we don't collect

This site has no user accounts, no login, and no backend server of its own. We don't collect names, emails, or the files you upload.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. More detail on how Google uses data from sites that use its services is available at How Google uses information from sites that use our services.
